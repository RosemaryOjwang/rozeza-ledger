# Competitor Analysis

## Purpose

This document analyzes existing accounting software solutions in Kenya and internationally to identify market opportunities for Rozeza Ledger.

The objective is not to copy competitors, but to understand the market and identify opportunities to build a differentiated product.

---

# Target Market

Rozeza Ledger is initially targeting:

- Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SMEs)
- Retail businesses
- Service businesses
- Schools
- Professional firms
- Growing startups

Future versions may support larger organizations and multiple industries.

---

# Competitors

## 1. QuickBooks

### Strengths

- Globally recognized
- Mature accounting features
- Large ecosystem
- Reliable reporting

### Weaknesses

- Can be expensive for SMEs
- Some localization requires additional configuration
- User interface can feel overwhelming for beginners

---

## 2. Zoho Books

### Strengths

- Modern interface
- Strong automation
- Good integrations
- Cloud-based

### Weaknesses

- Some advanced features require higher subscription tiers
- Limited customization for local accounting workflows

---

## 3. Sage

### Strengths

- Enterprise-grade accounting
- Strong financial reporting
- Trusted by many businesses

### Weaknesses

- Steeper learning curve
- Higher implementation cost

---

## 4. Odoo

### Strengths

- Modular ERP platform
- Accounting
- Inventory
- CRM
- HR
- Manufacturing

### Weaknesses

- Complex implementation
- May be more than many SMEs need

---

## 5. Uhasibu (Kenya)

### Strengths

- Built for Kenyan businesses
- Local tax support
- SME-focused

### Weaknesses

- Smaller ecosystem
- Limited advanced analytics

---

## 6. Qwan (Kenya)

### Strengths

- Cloud-based
- Kenyan tax support
- Easy to use

### Weaknesses

- Smaller feature ecosystem than global competitors
